A note from Robin

Hey Roman
and Isabella 👋

I'm Robin. I lead MONO, a small creative team in Sydney. We work with Main Sequence and a number of deep tech companies, helping them explain their ideas and document their work.

Earth AI caught my attention because there's so much to show, from the technology guiding your search to the people out in the field testing it. I'd love to learn more about what you're doing and how you're sharing that with the world.

I've included a few films and some motion work below to give you a feel for what we do. If any of it feels useful for Earth AI, it'd be great to have a chat.

Robin

01 — BMRG

BMRG

The Burnett Mary Regional Group (BMRG) commissioned us to document conservation work on Queensland's Mary River. We filmed everything from the drone footage to the interviews, bringing together the perspectives of scientists, Traditional Owners and landholders. The focus was the human story: the people working to protect the river and why it matters to them.
