What exactly do I receive for A$99?

A$99 includes GST. You receive one 30-second MP4 concept video and five high-resolution images covering one site and one proposed granny flat concept. We email a download link so you can share the files with your customer.

How much work do I have to do?

Email us clear photos of the site and a short description of the granny flat you’re proposing. Forward existing plans, surveys, quotes, finishes and visual references if you have them. No presentation or special form is needed.

Do I need finished plans?

No. Site photos and a clear idea are a useful starting point. Tell us any essential footprint, placement, exterior or access requirements. Details you haven’t specified will be interpreted, so supply plans when those details matter.

When do I receive the package?

Fast turnaround once we have your complete brief. If anything essential is missing, we’ll let you know before production begins.

What if I’m not happy with a detail?

One consolidated correction round is included to bring the concept into line with the original brief. Send your notes together by email. A new design or changed scope is quoted separately, with your approval before additional charges. Your applicable consumer rights remain unchanged.

Is this construction or planning accurate?

No. These are AI-assisted sales concepts, not measured drawings, architectural documentation, engineering, planning advice, approval assessments or construction plans. Check dimensions, access, services, feasibility and approval requirements through your normal design and approval process.

Can you match materials and finishes?

We use your references to guide the look. AI concepts may vary in fine detail, colour or geometry, so they shouldn’t be used as exact product or material specifications.

How does this help my proposal?

It gives your customer a visual to discuss alongside the price and scope. They can picture the granny flat on their site and share the direction with other decision-makers. It doesn’t guarantee a sale or approval.

Can I share these with my customer?

Yes. The package is intended for your customer-facing proposal. Make clear that the visuals are illustrative concepts and pair them with your confirmed scope, specifications and approval advice.

Will you publish my customer’s photos?

We will ask for separate permission before using supplied photos or project material in public examples. Please only send material you have permission to share for this work.

What if I need to cancel or delivery is late?

You can cancel for a full refund before production starts. If we miss the confirmed delivery deadline, you can choose to continue with a revised deadline or cancel for a full refund. Contact robin@monohq.co. Nothing in this offer limits your applicable rights under Australian Consumer Law.
