What’s the minimum order?

Orders start at 120 bottles. Tell us your preferred range and quantity and we’ll confirm the available options in your quote.

Can you help with the label design?

Yes. Our in-house design team can help prepare or adjust your artwork. Send your logo and any brand guidelines with your enquiry so we can discuss the right approach.

How long will my order take?

Timing depends on your bottle choice, order size, artwork and delivery location. Include your required date in your enquiry so the team can confirm a realistic production and delivery schedule.

Which bottle sizes can I choose?

VISY: 250ml, 350ml, 600ml and 1.5L. Boston: 300ml and 600ml. Active: 600ml. Square: 250ml and 600ml. The team will confirm availability when quoting.

What types of water do you offer?

The range includes spring, reverse osmosis (RO), distilled and alkaline water. Let us know your intended use and we’ll help you find a suitable option.

Do you work with trade partners?

Yes. We work with distributors, promotional product companies, printers, agencies and resellers. We support your customer relationship with manufacturing, artwork and delivery coordination.
